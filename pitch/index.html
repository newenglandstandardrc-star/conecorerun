The concept

Cannonball Run meets
your local Stewart’s

Players race to visit every Stewart’s Shops location across the network. A hidden QR code inside each store is the check-in mechanism — you have to walk in and browse to find it. No purchase required, but you have to be there.

🗺️

Hidden QR check-in

A QR code hidden somewhere inside every store. Placement rotates periodically. Staff are in on the secret. You have to actually browse to find it.

🏆

Three race stages

County heat → State heat → Full network. First to complete each stage wins. Seasonal races tied to local events keep it fresh year-round.

🌲

Pioneer bonus

Visiting a low-traffic, underserved location earns extra Scoops. Drives foot traffic to exactly the stores that need it most.

🥛

Dairy bonus

Honor-system tap confirming a dairy purchase — milk, eggs, or ice cream. Reinforces what makes Stewart’s uniquely Stewart’s.

❤️

Loyal bonus

Check in near a competitor and earn bonus Scoops for choosing Stewart’s. The competitor is never named — just quietly rewarded against.

🍦

Scoops economy

Points called Scoops — redeemable for free coffee, a single scoop, a hand-packed pint, or an ice cream cake. No cash value. Ever.

Safety & privacy

Built safe
from the ground up

ConeCoreRun operates in rural upstate New York, Vermont, and New Hampshire — areas where survivors of domestic violence may have limited access to services. A predictable check-in pattern could put someone at serious risk. These aren’t afterthoughts. They’re foundations.

97%

of DV service providers report technology-facilitated abuse in their cases. Gaming apps are explicitly identified platforms. — NNEDV Safety Net

🚫
No live location sharing. Competitors never see where you are or where you’ve been. Minimum 24-hour delay on any shared activity.
📊
Stats only. Other players see your score and badges. Never which stores you visited, never when, never in what order.
🔇
The quiet exit. One tap. No explanation. No visible signal to other players. No trace. Always accessible from the Profile screen.
🤝
Wellspring partnership. Stewart’s existing charitable partner in Saratoga County. 24-hour hotline: 518-584-8188. Surfaced quietly in-app — always there, never intrusive.
⚖️
NNEDV Safety Net review required before any location-adjacent feature ships. VAWA compliance. Vermont Safe at Home considered in account creation flow.

The rewards

Earn Scoops.
Redeem in-store.

Points are called Scoops — rooted in Stewart’s dairy identity. No cash value. Never purchasable. Redeemable for Stewart’s products only. The stakes should never be high enough for a player to compromise good decision making.
